# Characters Unlimited

Character creation and advancement for Rifts and Heroes Unlimited. Palladium Fantasy RPG is deferred.

## Language

**Manual adjustment**:
A player-entered departure from a character rule or calculated value, permitted on the honor system without a GM access gate, required reason, or override marker.
_Avoid_: GM approval

**Generation rules**:
The selected rules for rolling a character's initial attributes, including any configured reroll-ones or extra-die/drop-lowest options.
_Avoid_: Attribute bonuses

**Rule pack**:
A reviewed collection of character options and rules drawn from identified source books. Its source references identify the origin of its contents.
_Avoid_: Raw book, Markdown book

**Saved character**:
The authoritative character record maintained by the utility, including selections, calculated values, and overrides.
_Avoid_: Exported sheet

**Exported sheet**:
An editable PDF representation of a saved character. Changes made directly to it remain in that PDF.
_Avoid_: Saved character

**Advancement**:
A character's progression to a higher level, including the resulting bonuses and eligible new selections.
_Avoid_: Live session tracking

**Additive adjustment**:
A manual amount added to a calculated value that continues to change as the character advances.
_Avoid_: Fixed value

**Fixed value**:
A manually specified replacement that stays constant when the underlying calculated value changes.
_Avoid_: Additive adjustment

**Equipped item**:
An item currently worn or wielded by the character, subject to the applicable equipment rules.
_Avoid_: Stored item

**Stored item**:
An owned item not currently worn or wielded. Its location determines whether it contributes to the character's carried load.
_Avoid_: Equipped item

**Custom entry**:
A character-specific skill, power, weapon, armor, or equipment item supplied by the player with explicit values and effects.
_Avoid_: Rule pack

**Missing dependency**:
A rule or character option referenced by a supplied book whose necessary definition is absent from the supplied sources.
_Avoid_: Implemented rule

**Automation gap**:
A character mechanic the utility cannot yet calculate or validate from accepted rules. Manual completion does not remove the gap.
_Avoid_: Full automation

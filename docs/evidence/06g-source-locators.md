All Communications skill values/context: Cryptography 25%+5, requires Literacy; normally study two hours, or attempt after ten minutes at −30%. Laser 30%+5, requires Radio Basic, Electrical Engineer, Computer Operation. Optic Systems 30%+5; selecting both it and TV/Video adds a one-time +5% to TV/Video. Surveillance Systems 30%+5; requires Basic Electronics or Electrical Engineering; Computer Operation and Literacy are needed only for complex high-tech systems. Tailing and equipment/hidden-device failure are all within this one Surveillance definition/base; no separate Rogue Tailing import is needed. TV/Video 25%+4. Read Sensory Equipment 30%+5. Radio Basic 45%+5 and expressly excludes repairs; Radio Scramblers 35%+5; Radio Satellite 25%+5. The three Radio definitions are on the `f1f...` Markdown span above, but original printed p.50/PDF51 resolves their category.

Ambiguity: fixed Radio Basic, Radio Scrambler and TV/Video also belong to the one-choice Communications category. The page does not say whether the choice must be distinct from those fixed grants or whether a duplicate fills the choice; general restriction item 10 only says duplicated skills across programs use the highest bonus. T.V./Video and Radio Scrambler(s) are minor source-name variants. Optic/TV synergy is explicit and should be modeled as the +5% contribution once when both are present.

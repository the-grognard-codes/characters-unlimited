# Powers Unlimited 3 — canonical identity discovery (not mechanics review)

## Finding

The original Contents lists 46 Minor entries and 79 Major headings, including the Animal Abilities extension (not an independent power). Excluding that extension leaves 124 named powers; adding the ten new animal-type choices gives 134 if the book counts those variants as abilities, a plausible but not explicit reconciliation of the foreword total. This is source-identity discovery only: no numerical rules, dependencies, eligibility, or acceptance were reviewed. Candidate IDs below are from active `source-inventory.json` unless marked as a shared Contents candidate; these are identity-source locators, not mechanical acceptance.

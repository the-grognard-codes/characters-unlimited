Secondary category eligibility on printed 47 / PDF 48:

- Communications: Radio: Basic and TV/Video only.
- Electrical: Basic Electronics only.
- Domestic: any. Espionage: Wilderness Survival only. Mechanical: Basic Mechanics and Auto Mechanics only.
- Medical: First Aid or Holistic Medicine; Holistic Medicine costs two selections. Military: Recognize Weapon Quality only.
- Physical: any except Acrobatics, Gymnastics, Boxing, and Wrestling. Hand to Hand: Expert costs two selections; Martial Arts (or Assassin if evil) costs three.
- Pilot Basic: any; Pilot Advanced: none; Pilot Related: none.
- Rogue: any except Computer Hacking, Find Contraband, and Safecracking. Science: Astronomy and Math skills only. Technical: any.
- W.P. Ancient Weapons: any. W.P. Modern Weapons: any except energy weapons. Wilderness Skills: any.

The page defines eligibility and slot costs but does not grant these Secondary entries automatically. First Aid has no explicit failed-roll rule here; do not import the Paramedic retry/treatment procedure. Holistic Medicine's “twice as quick” healing is narrative/source guidance, not a quantified percentage or extra check.

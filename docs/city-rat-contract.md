# 05C2: Human City Rat foundation

Parent tickets 02, 05, 07, 08, 13 and 19 remain open. This slice activates the Human City Rat O.C.C. from Rifts Ultimate Edition printed pp. 88–89 alongside the existing Human Vagabond.

## Behavior and seams

CharacterApplication creation, choices, projection, advancement, portable save and editable PDF are the public verification seams already approved for this project. Shared skill identities retain one exact immutable pack pin. A class profile replaces allowances, required grants, combat entitlement, resources and advancement; it cannot replace the pack identity or shared skill catalog. Old immutable packs continue projecting their original Vagabond rules.

City Rat receives only its own attribute bonus (Spd +1D6+1), automatic Running, +3 Perception and +2D4 S.D.C. Running is acquired once at creation, persists in portable saves and does not multiply when also selected as an optional skill. Starting HP records effective P.E. plus 1D6, as explicitly confirmed by the user; later attribute changes do not rewrite the initial contribution. General S.D.C. is 2D6+12, plus the class and acquired Physical contributions.

Required percentile grants and named language/vehicle choices follow pp. 88–89. The class has ten Related and eight Secondary choices initially. One proficiency from either weapon family fills its required slot; duplicates remain visible without filling another slot. Related gains occur at levels 2, 4, 6, 9 and 12; Secondary gains occur at 3, 6, 10 and 15. The shared City Rat/Vagabond XP table is p. 295. Existing learned-level growth, recorded HP dice, undo/recovery and exact historic pins apply through level fifteen.

## Explicitly unfinished

This is not full mechanical review of the canonical O.C.C. Required implants, equipment, credits, broader skill/W.P. choices, subtype dependencies and conditional activities remain pending. The class P.P.E. bonus modifies a general base; its age-22 halving requires age/base/rounding work. Counting Hand to Hand upgrades toward the minimum three Physical/Rogue Related selections requires source resolution. The foundation displays these gaps and does not invent rules.

## Validation

Source-worked public examples use constant die faces of four: MA12, PS12, PE13, SPD33, starting HP17 and S.D.C.32. Native tongue92%, literacy55%, Barter49% (including Basic Math and Literacy synergies) and Tailing50% apply at level one with IQ12. A level-three Hovercycle grant learned at level one is86%. Browser class selection, autosave/reopen and rendered editable PDF are required in addition to automated checks and independent Standards/Spec reviews before merge.

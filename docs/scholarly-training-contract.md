# Source-bound scholarly training and equipment

Rifts class declarations may opt into `higher_advancement.related_per_award` and `secondary_per_award`. Each is a bounded positive whole number (1-1000), with the existing source-bearing advancement declaration and distinct award-level list. Omission preserves the original one-choice-per-level behavior. The effective allowance counts awards through the current level, so undo restores the earlier allowance without changing learned skill dates.

Combat declarations may provide exact `proficiency_counts` with `ancient` and `modern` whole numbers between zero and1000. This is separate from the existing combined-family allowance. Optional `additional_proficiency_cost` specifies a bounded related-choice cost for each distinct nonfixed proficiency beyond the required eligible slots. Duplicates do not multiply cost or fill required slots; ineligible training consumes an additional choice without satisfying a required slot. Existing declarations retain their original allowance/count behavior. Starting groups show actual saved training; eligibility and excess skills remain honor-system guidance.

No Hand to Hand Combat Skill is an ordinary source-bearing style definition, with zero cost and the reviewed printed347 starting/progression values. Scholar and Scientist start there; their available paid styles declare their own costs. The builder reads those declarations rather than hardcoding the Vagabond's upgrade costs. Conditional action resolution remains descriptive.

A starting-equipment group may declare `repeat_for_proficiencies` as a positive minimum number of slots instead of `proficiency_slot`. It requires explicit training catalogs and option requirements. The shared compiler creates stable numbered group IDs and names, one per distinct saved ancient-then-modern proficiency, with at least the declared minimum. Every acquired numbered receipt remains available when training is removed or reordered. Current requirements can show a mismatch, while original possession identities, selected items and dice remain intact. Groups and generated identities are bounded to1000; declaration/expanded identity collisions reject. Unselected owned declarations preflight before initial dice.

Rogue Scholar and Rogue Scientist use these shared contracts. Their new profiles replace every supported class-owned skill, resource and equipment field and preserve older immutable archives. Required automatically acquired skills do not fill additional related category minimums. Percentile bonuses use actual acquisition dates and the highest applicable required training once. Scholar Restoration applies its ten-point bonus only to related Art, Calligraphy, Forgery and Photography. Scientist Analyze/Hypothesize add their ordinary skill bonuses to acquired matching reviewed skills; focused analysis Perception and item-specific contraband bonuses remain source descriptions.

The representative equipment catalog supports each selected available proficiency through its own receipt. Scholar's source vehicle supports Automobile and Hover Craft through representative commercial jeep/hover truck options. Scientist's optional specimen kit retains its D4 jar count; one independent starting ammunition receipt grants two spare clips total. Unknown prices, weights, vehicle durability and device statistics are unspecified. Entomological Medicine, Zoology, broader proficiencies/equipment and nonhuman racial paths remain source-coverage work; this batch does not certify the full amended build.

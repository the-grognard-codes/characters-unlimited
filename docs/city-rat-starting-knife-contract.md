# 08B7: City Rat starting knife

Use the approved CharacterApplication, persistence/portable/history, HTTP and editable-PDF verification seams. Equipment1.10 adds a separate starting knife group to City Rat. One Large Knife (1D6 S.D.C.) is granted free, carried and unequipped, with its own original receipt. City Rat p.89/PDF92 supplies the entitlement; Basic Gear p.262/PDF265 supplies the existing Large Knife definition. Its price range20–100 is a catalog purchase price; this starting grant spends nothing. Weight remains unknown. No W.P. or other training is granted.

The armor group definition remains byte-for-byte canonically equivalent so acquired armor receipts upgrade without migration. Knife and armor can be granted in either order and independently; funds/fixed gear, attributes, resources, training and advancement remain unchanged. Manual inventory edits/removal cannot reset original grant availability. Old1.9 City Rat pins have no knife group until an explicit reviewed update. Other classes retain their existing choices.

Public worked example: a City Rat with2400 credits, six fixed gear rows and Urban Warrior armor adds one knife for eight inventory rows,11lb known weight and eight unknown-weight units. Equip the knife to get existing melee attack calculations, retaining untrained status until a player separately selects W.P. Knife. Portable/reopen/advance/undo and native editable weapon fields retain receipt and behavior. Existing Small Knife1D4 is not an allowed starting option under the class1D6 requirement. Full weapons, clips, transport, implants and City Rat mechanical acceptance remain open.

Source text says the power gives a humanoid “traits, abilities and instincts” of an animal and “does not actually transform” them (MD 15406). The Random Animal Type Table at MD 15410–15433 offers ten selectable profiles: Fish; Cat, small/domestic; Bear; Mustelid; Canine; Cat, big/wild; Bird; Nocturnal; Hoofed; Reptile. These are variants/options beneath the one power, not ten races. Specific type descriptions follow at MD 15435 onward. Mustelid explicitly includes wolverine, badger, ferret, ermine, and weasel (MD 15542); Hoofed includes horses, deer, antelope, buffalo, cattle, and sheep (MD 15522). The two Cat labels are distinct type options, not aliases. `Animal Metamorphosis` begins at MD 15571 and is a separate power.
